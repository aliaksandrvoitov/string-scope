THE SHORT VERSION

String Scope does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

DETAILS

  • Your compositions are saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are never uploaded anywhere by String Scope.
  • The app does not use the internet, location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • No third-party SDKs, analytics or advertising frameworks are included.
  • If you email support, your message is used only to reply to you.
